Shipping & Delivery Policy

Delivery area, timings, and failed delivery guidelines

1. Delivery Radius & Service Zone

Atithi Bhoj operates from a single home kitchen in Kalyanpur, Kanpur (208017). Delivery is strictly limited to a 5 km radius from our kitchen to ensure food arrives hot and fresh. Active zones include Kalyanpur, IIT Kanpur Campus, CSJM University Campus, Kakadeo, and Rawatpur.

Tiffins are delivered twice daily during the following delivery slots:

Doorstep delivery is 100% FREE on all subscription plans and trial orders within our 5km service radius in Kanpur.
